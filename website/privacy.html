i
 Overview

Lore ("we", "us", or "our") is a private personal journal and emotional companion app developed by GX Devs. This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you use Lore and how your privacy is protected.

Your private thoughts, voice recordings, and emotional reflections belong solely to you. Lore is designed around an on-device architecture: we do not upload, collect, or sell your journal entries or personal data.

Package ID: com.gxdevs.lore  ·  Platform: Android (Google Play)

S
 100% On-Device Data & AI Processing

All core journaling features in Lore operate entirely offline and locally on your device. No text, audio, or sentiment data is ever transmitted to remote developer servers.

› Journal Text & Audio Recordings — Stored exclusively in local encrypted database storage on your device.
› On-Device Emotion & Sentiment AI — Sentiment scoring and language processing happen locally without cloud API dependencies.
› Companion Evolution & Relics — Companion growth, stage unlocks, and achievements are saved locally.
› Offline Capability — You can use all core journaling and companion evolution features in Airplane Mode.
T
 Third-Party Services & Google Cloud Sync

Lore integrates standard Google Play services for optional in-app purchases, authentication, and voluntary Google Drive backups.

G
Google Play In-App Billing
Processes payments for optional Lore Premium subscriptions or digital items. Financial information is handled directly by Google Play.

A
Google Identity Services
Used optionally for account authentication. Only basic profile info (email, account name) is authenticated via Google.
View Google Privacy Policy →
D
Google Drive Backup Sync
Allows users to manually trigger encrypted backups of their journal database to their own Google Drive account. Lore accesses only app-created backup files (appDataFolder / drive.file) and does not access non-app Google Drive files.
View Google Privacy Policy →

Lore contains no third-party advertising SDKs or tracking networks.

P
 App Permissions

Lore requests only the following permissions to enable user-initiated features:

› RECORD_AUDIO — Required only when you record a voice journal entry. Audio is saved locally.
› USE_BIOMETRIC / USE_FINGERPRINT — Enables hardware biometric lock to secure your sanctuary.
› POST_NOTIFICATIONS — Used to send local scheduled reminders for daily reflection.
› BILLING — Enables Google Play in-app purchases for Lore Premium.
› READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / CAMERA — Required only when attaching custom photos to journal entries.
L
 App Lock & Privacy Controls
› Biometric & PIN Security — Lock Lore with a custom PIN or device biometrics.
› Decoy PIN Vault — Enter an alternate PIN to present a clean decoy workspace.
› Screenshot Blackout — Prevents screen capture and obscures recent app switcher previews.
X
 Account & Data Deletion Instructions

You have full control over your data. To request deletion of your account or associated data, follow these simple steps:

1. Local Data & Journals: Uninstalling Lore immediately deletes all local journal entries, audio recordings, settings, and companion progress from your device.
2. Account & Backup Deletion Request: Send an email to gxdevsstudio@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request". Include your registered account email. All associated records will be permanently deleted within 7 business days.

Contact gxdevsstudio@gmail.com for any account deletion requests.

C
 Children's Privacy

Lore is intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. All data created within the app remains exclusively on the local device.
