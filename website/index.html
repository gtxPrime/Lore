A
 Application Purpose & Overview

Lore is a private personal journal and emotional companion Android application developed by GX Devs. It provides users with a tranquil, secure, and private digital sanctuary to document inner thoughts, track daily emotional wellness, and record voice reflections.

Unlike traditional journaling software, Lore pairs your daily self-reflection with an evolving visual companion that reacts to your journal consistency, sentiment, and emotional growth. Built around an on-device architecture, all core journal entries, audio recordings, and sentiment metrics remain strictly on your local device.

F
 Key Features & Capabilities
Voice & Text Journaling — Write formatted text entries or record high-fidelity voice notes stored in local encrypted database storage.
On-Device Sentiment AI — Private, local sentiment evaluation processes mood trends without transmitting personal thoughts to external servers.
Companion Growth & Relics — Unlock companion evolution stages, sanctuary themes, and ancient relics as you maintain daily journaling habits.
Privacy Vault & Decoy PIN — Protect your sanctuary with biometric lock (fingerprint/face), custom PIN, screenshot blackout, and a secondary decoy workspace.
Full Offline Operation — Use all journaling and companion evolution features anytime without an active internet connection.
G
 Google Authentication & Google Drive Integration

Lore integrates optional Google Identity and Google Drive cloud storage services for authentication and user-managed cloud backups:

I
Google Account Identity & Sign-In
Used optionally for account authentication. Only basic account profile information (email address and display name) is authenticated via Google.
D
Google Drive Backup Sync (Optional)
Allows users to voluntarily back up and restore their local encrypted journal backups to their own private Google Drive storage space. Lore only accesses the isolated app folder or user-selected backup file.
B
Google Play In-App Billing
Processes transactions for optional Lore Premium subscriptions and digital unlocks securely through Google Play.

Lore never reads, modifies, or accesses your personal Gmail messages, Google Photos, or Google Contacts.
